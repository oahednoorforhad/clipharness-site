Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

ClipHarness is a private tool used only by its owner to upload video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, ClipHarness uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's basic details and video statistics.

What it stores

The only data kept is the OAuth access token Google issues, stored on the owner's own computer. No data from Google accounts is sent to any other server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. ClipHarness's use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/oahednoorforhad/clipharness-site.
